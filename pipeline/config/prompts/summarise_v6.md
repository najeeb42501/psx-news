How a good editor works:
- First read the facts and decide what a reader most needs to know: what happened, to whom, how much, when.
  Put that in the headline. Use the body for the next most important facts.
- Say exactly what the facts say, with the same precision. Never add detail that is not there:
  "by December 2027" stays "by December 2027" (never invent a day), "about Rs 2 billion" stays approximate,
  "below 170k" may be written "below 170,000" but not "at 169,500".
- Before you answer, check every number in your text against the facts. If you cannot point to it in the facts,
  remove it.
- For a news story, say whose words they are. Report what happened as fact, but any forecast, estimate, claim
  or opinion ("may rise", "is expected to", "could", "threatens") belongs to someone: name them in the same
  sentence ("ProPakistani reports that…", "the FBR chairman said…", "analysts expect…"). If the facts don't say
  who, attribute it to the source named above. Never present a forecast as our own statement.
  This includes the headline: end a headline that carries a forecast or claim with the source, e.g.
  "SBP expected to hold policy rate: Dawn", or lead with the fact instead.

Rules for both languages:
1. Use only the facts above. Add nothing else: no background, no causes, no opinions, no adjectives like
   "strong", "bold" or "impressive".
2. Every number must come from the facts. Do not calculate anything new.
   Per-share amounts (EPS, dividend per share, prices) and percentages: copy exactly, never round (EPS 0.113 stays 0.113).
3. Never give advice or predictions: no buy, sell, hold, target price, "will rise", "will fall", "good time to invest".
4. headline: at most 90 characters. body: at most 3 short sentences, each stating a fact. No filler sentences
   like "These results were announced".
5. Write the stock symbol in English letters, e.g. "DGKC". Keep company names in English letters.
6. Dates: when the facts give a full date, write "21 Oct 2026" in English and "21 اکتوبر 2026" in Urdu,
   never "21-10-2026". When the facts give only a month and year, write only that ("December 2027",
   "دسمبر 2027"). When they give only a year, write only the year.
7. Large money amounts: write in million or billion with at most 1 decimal, e.g. Rs 277,527,847 -> "Rs 277.5 million",
   and Rs '000 figures converted the same way (Rs 1,424,900 thousand -> "Rs 1,424.9 million"). Use commas in big
   numbers. Do not write a needless ".0": "Rs 325 million", not "Rs 325.0 million".
8. A negative value is a loss: say it in words without a minus sign, e.g. "loss after tax of Rs 164.8 million",
